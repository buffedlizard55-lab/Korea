# 🚀 Start Here — The 5-Minute Beginner's Guide

New to this guide (or new to Korea)? Start here. This page assumes **zero prior knowledge** and explains the 2 rules, the 3 deal types, and what to do in your first 48 hours.

> **Trip: Oct 31 – Nov 22, 2026 (Sat → Sun) | US Citizens | No ARC | Last verified: 2026-08-03**

---

## 📖 The 2 Rules That Make Everything Easy

**Rule 1 — Skip anything that needs `본인인증` (bon-in-in-jeung).**
That's Korea's phone-based identity check. Korean loyalty apps (Starbucks KR, Outback, CJ ONE, Happy Point, Coupang Eats) demand it, and **it requires a Korean resident ID (RRN/ARC) you don't have**. If an app asks for a Korean phone number for "verification," close it and use a ✅ deal from this guide instead. You'll see `📱` / `🪪` badges marking these to avoid.

**Rule 2 — Your passport stays in your pocket for food.**
Korean restaurants, cafes, kiosks, markets, and delivery apps **never** check ID when you scan a coupon, barcode, or QR code. Your passport is only needed for: **(1)** buying alcohol, **(2)** hypermarket tax refunds (Lotte Mart / E-Mart, spend ≥₩15,000), **(3)** boarding-pass discounts and airport lounges.

| Type | Icon | Example | How you claim it |

**Finding everything:** Google Maps barely works in Korea — install **Naver Map** (English mode) and use the [tap-to-navigate links](../docs/everyday-savings.md) for every chain.

## 🗓️ Your First 48 Hours, Step by Step

**Day 0 — Oct 31 (Sat, arrival)**
1. Land, get through immigration, grab a **T-money card** or use **WOWPASS** (prepaid KRW card; also earns cashback at GS25 5%, Paik's Coffee 3%, Compose Coffee 5%, Dunkin 5%, Lotte Mart 2%…).
2. Check into your hotel. Evening: **convenience-store run** (CU / GS25 / 7-Eleven / emart24) — it's the *last day* of October's 1+1/2+1 promos. Look for red "1+1" / yellow "2+1" shelf tags.
3. Order late-night chicken via **Shuttle** (English, US card) or **Baemin Guest Checkout**. No tipping.

**Day 1 — Nov 1 (Sun)**
4. All 4 convenience chains **reset to fresh November promos** today — breakfast gimbap + ₩1,500–2,000 iced Americano (Mega/Compose/Paik's/The Venti) for under $5.
5. Open **KakaoTalk** and add the brand channels — instant coupons in chat, scan at kiosks.
6. For lunch: **No Brand Burger** (₩2,500 Amazing Bulgogi) or **Mom's Touch** (set ~₩7,600) in Myeongdong/DDP/Gangnam/Hongdae.

**Day 2+**
- Check the [trip calendar](trip-calendar.md) each morning for what's on that day.
- Use the [neighborhood guide](neighborhood-guide.md) when you're hungry and unsure where to go.
- Before any birthday: sign up **IKEA Family** ≥7 days ahead (free apple tart coupon by email) — and bring your passport in case the restaurant asks to verify (Korean guides say ID may be requested for the birthday freebie; the free weekday hot drink needs no ID).
