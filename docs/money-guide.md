# 💳 Money Guide — Paying, Exchanging & Budgeting in Korea

How to pay, where to get cash, and what things cost — so you never overpay or get stuck without a payment method.

> **Last verified: 2026-08-03** · Also see: [Arrival Guide](arrival-guide.md) · [FAQ](faq.md)

---

## 💳 1. Payment Methods — What to Carry

| Method | Best for | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **US credit card** (Visa/MC) | Kiosks, restaurants, delivery, department stores | Wide acceptance; bring a **no-foreign-transaction-fee** card. Amex is accepted less often. |
| **WOWPASS** (prepaid KRW) | Everywhere + transit + cashback | Mastercard-backed; top up from US card or cash; **partner cashback** (verified Aug 2026): GS25 5%, Compose Coffee 5%, Dunkin 5%, Paik's 3%, Twosome 3%, The Venti 3%, Kyochon 2%, Lotte Mart 2%, E-Mart 2%, Olive Young 4%, Daiso 5%. [wowpass.io](https://www.wowpass.io) |
| **T-money** | Subway/bus | ₩4,000 card + top-up at convenience stores; some prefer WOWPASS (does both) |
| **Cash (KRW)** | Markets, street stalls, Tongin coins, small shops | Carry ₩50,000–100,000 at a time |

**The golden rule:** kiosks and chains take cards; traditional markets and coin lunchboxes want cash. Never rely on one method alone.

## 💱 2. Where to Get Cash (best rates first)

1. **Myeongdong exchange shops** — the classic tourist move; compare 2–3 shops on the main street, rates usually beat the airport. 🟡 widely documented
2. **WOWPASS machines** — self-service, decent rates, no counter lines.
3. **Airport exchange desks** — fine for a small first amount only (worst rates).
4. **ATM withdrawal** — use Global ATMs (e.g., at banks/convenience stores); **always choose "KRW" not "USD"** at the machine to avoid Dynamic Currency Conversion fees.

## 🚫 3. Never Do These

- ❌ **Tip** — Korea has no tipping; the menu price is final (delivery drivers, taxis, hotels included).
- ❌ **Pay in USD** at merchants offering it — you get a bad conversion rate. Always pay in KRW.
- ❌ **Exchange at the airport** more than a starter amount.
- ❌ **Assume restaurant meals are tax-refundable** — they never are (see [Arrival Guide](arrival-guide.md)).
