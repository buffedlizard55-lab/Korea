## Couple payment rules

1. Do not put both travelers’ cards in one wallet.
2. Use the card with the best exchange/foreign-transaction terms, but keep a backup.
3. Separate transit money from restaurant/grocery money mentally or in the tracker.
4. Do not load a payment product solely for small cashback unless it already fits your route.
5. Record the actual exchange/payment result in [Couple Budget](couple-budget.md) when it materially affects the cost.

## Before a large purchase

- [ ] Final price visible in KRW
- [ ] Tax-refund eligibility confirmed if relevant
- [ ] Passport present if immediate refund is planned
- [ ] Normal-card fallback works
- [ ] No minimum-spend purchase is being forced just for a reward
