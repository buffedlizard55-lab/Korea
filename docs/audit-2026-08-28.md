# Registry audit — 2026-08-28

> **Last verified: 2026-08-28**

## Scope and result

This is the tenth review pass over `data/deals.csv`, and it is the first one aimed at the part of the brief the earlier passes under-served: free offers, BOGO, welcome and sign-up promotions, first-time-customer terms and any promotional discount, inside **Seoul and Busan only**. Every line was read against the source in its own `source_url` column or was explicitly marked as not re-opened. Ten live verification rounds were run against official government, city-tourism, palace-administration, duty-free and operator pages.

**Honesty note about method.** The sandbox has no outbound network access from the shell, so `scripts/check_links.py` cannot be used as evidence of anything, and no claim in this repo was validated with `curl`. Every statement below comes from a page that was actually opened through the retrieval tool on 2026-08-28 and read directly. Where a page did not publish a term, the record says the term is unpublished instead of filling the gap; where only press coverage supports a number, the tier is `corroborated`, not `official`.

## What changed

1. **20 new records** (14 Seoul, 6 Busan), each with the terms quoted from the page, a recheck date, a local backup, and a manual link in `docs/core-deal-index.md` and `docs/verification-log.md`. Five are Korea Tourism Organization VISITKOREA Exclusive partner offers whose percentages and codes were previously withheld because no current page existed; two promote records the 2026-08-19 sweep explicitly declined (Yacht Tale percentage, Unhyeongung fee) now that both are published; three palace rules and two free-admission venues come from the Cultural Heritage Administration and Seoul Tourism Organization pages.
2. **8 retained records corrected** — see the `corrected`/`flagged`/`amended` rows in the table below. Two were overstatements in our own wording (the bus 20% headline, the "10% food-hall" line in two guides), one was a dead source domain, one was a live event that ends before the trip, three shared a single Korean-language citation, and one cited a page that no longer exists.
3. **16 unsupported claims re-labelled to match the evidence** — 8 KakaoTalk brand-channel rows in `docs/signup-welcome-deals.md` claimed 🟢 official with no link and are now 🟡 in-app check; 4 cells in `docs/verification-log.md` (convenience shelf offers, Korea Sale Festa, Cheonan taxi, Daejeon tour) lost their 🟢; the Klook "100% Tourist / zero ID check" cell became 🟡; and the "10% off food-hall" claim carried in `docs/birthday-freebies.md` and `docs/us-tourist-action-plan.md` was replaced with the voucher terms the official pages actually publish.
4. **Discovery queue updated** — 10 new leads recorded, including 6 rejected with reasons so they are not re-hunted, and the three previously-candidated records are marked promoted.

## Irregularities flagged for review

Nothing below was silently fixed where a human decision is required. Items marked **decision needed** change what the couple can budget.

1. **Contradiction between two official pages — Culture Day at the palaces.** The Royal Palaces and Tombs Center fee page still grants free admission on "the last Wednesday of each month", while the Cultural Heritage Administration Culture Day portal states that from 2026-04-01 Culture Day **moved to every Wednesday**. For Nov 4/11/18 2026 the two pages give opposite answers for Gyeongbokgung, Changdeokgung, Deoksugung and Changgyeonggung. Captured as `SEOUL-PALACE-LASTWED-FREE` (live-check) — **decision needed: phone or re-open the Palace Center page in the week of travel rather than assume a Wednesday is free.**
2. **A source domain we relied on was hijacked.** `koreasalefesta.kr`, carried since 2026-08-18 as the official festival source and still classified `official` in `data/source-policy.csv`, returned a contact-lens manufacturer login page ("WELCOME TO MEDIOS") on 2026-08-28. `data/source-policy.csv` still lists the domain as official — **decision needed: remove or demote the policy row.**
3. **Two live records on a JS-only operator page.** Namsangol Hanok Village (`SEOUL-NAMSANGOL-FREE`) publishes hours only through scripts, and independent guides split between a Monday and a Tuesday closure; the record follows the official Visit Seoul page (Tuesday). `hanokmaeul.co.kr` is registered in the policy file as a source we cannot actually read — keep as corroborating only.
4. **Percentages that only the press publishes.** `SEOUL-LOTTE-TOURIST-MEMBERSHIP` is tier `corroborated`: the Lotte global site confirms the promotion exists and its Jan 2–Dec 31 window, but the 5/7/10/20% benefit table is only in two April 2026 business-press articles. `BUSAN-VBP-REVISIT` has the same shape — the official pass site runs the application flow but does not print the ₩10,000/₩20,000 amounts or the Jul 28–Oct 31 window, which come from the platform listing. **Neither should be budgeted as cash.**
5. **Every "partner discount" on both city-tour bus pages is gated on a paid same-day ticket.** Seoul lists 21 partners, Busan lists about 16; none is a standalone deal, and both pages say discounts are non-combinable and valid only on the boarding day (Busan: receipt + up to 3–4 companions, valid until the next day). Only five of those offers became registry rows, chosen for size (₩10,000 off, 50% off, 25% off, 30% off, membership upgrade); the remaining eleven are listed inside `SEOUL-CITYBUS-MOMSTOUCH-NAMSAN` notes and in the verification log rather than promoted, to avoid inflating the "deals" count with 10%-off-a-noodle-bowl items.
6. **One operator page is mis-translated, so it cannot be quoted verbatim.** The Busan City Tour English affiliate page renders the aquarium child ticket as "Postman 26,500", the Skyline Luge as "Skyline Rouge", and the Songdo cable car as "BUSAN AR CRUSE". Venue naming in `BUSAN-CITYTOUR-SEALIFE` follows the aquarium itself, not the operator page; the other two were left out of the registry for this reason and are only noted in the log.
7. **`live_check=yes` is on every row** (166 of 166), which makes the column decorative. The real signal is `status` (live-check 64). **decision needed: retire the column or define it as "a human re-opened the page today".
8. **Recheck dates are mass-produced.** 126 of 166 rows carry the identical `expiry_or_recheck` 2026-10-17 — three weeks into a trip that runs Oct 31–Nov 22 — so the blanket value cannot be read as a per-record check schedule. All 20 new rows were given per-record dates (2026-12-31 for VISITKOREA codes, 2026-11-30/2026-10-31 for date-bounded promos, 2026-10-17 for standing free admission) so the divergence is visible.
9. **Single-source citations still standing.** 9 source URLs are each shared by 2 or more rows (Busan skywalks were de-shared today; `BUSAN-BOKBYEONGSAN`/`BUSAN-MANGYANG-EXHIBIT` still share one December 2022 Visit Busan feature, and the Lotte Mart and Lotte World rows share one page). Two rows cite only a blog domain (`motoworldexplorer.com`) and two convenience rows cite the CU/GS25 homepage rather than a promo page — those need per-venue URLs before they can be called verified.
10. **24 records are outside the stated Seoul/Busan scope** (Nationwide 22, Daejeon 5, Cheonan 3, Incheon 2 minus overlap of national chains used in both cities) and one of them, `DAEJEON-SUMMER50`, is expired for this window, while `NATIONWIDE-TWOSOME` is a rejected claim kept on purpose. `SEOUL-GARDEN-SHOW-2026` is labelled Seoul but its venue, Nanjido, is in Goyang — and it closes 2026-10-27, before arrival. **decision needed: keep out-of-scope rows for context, or move them to a separate file.**
11. **Four data files that the docs present as live are header-only**: `data/deal-tracker.csv`, `data/branch-options.csv`, `data/couple-budget.csv`, `data/hotel-shortlist.csv`. Nothing references them programmatically, so the empty state is invisible to the checkers but misleading to a reader.
12. **The promotion windows that were excluded are worth recording as decisions, not omissions:** Seoul Welcome Week 2026 ran May 1–8; Seoul Beauty Week 2026 ran Aug 22–25; Busan One Asia Festival 2026 moved to June 27–28 (June 20 park concert); the KTO ₩5,000 express-bus foreigner fare window ran Jun 15–Jul 14; the 2025 Korea Grand Sale retailer offers and the Wehome Busan 10% code `BORAHAE` both expired (Jun 19). None is usable Oct 31–Nov 22, and none was added.
13. **No true product-level BOGO exists for foreign tourists in the window.** Ten rounds found no official 1+1/2+1 term for Seoul or Busan covering Oct 31–Nov 22 2026. The closest verified offers are free-gift-with-purchase items: The Painters welcome gift set, the Jinyeon lucky pouch, a free WAKEMAKE cushion at ₩100,000 in Starfield Avenue, and a free drink at ₩20,000+ on the Busan tour-partner list. Convenience-store 1+1 SKUs are set monthly and are not published far enough ahead to verify, so they stay an in-store shelf-tag check, not a deal.
14. **Records whose eligibility gate is not money.** `SEOUL-MOTORSTUDIO-LOUNGE-VK` and the other VISITKOREA rows require a free VISITKOREA membership and, on several pages, non-Korean citizenship; the palace age waiver needs passport proof of date of birth; duty-free needs a passport and an outbound ticket. Every one of these is reachable by a US tourist without an ARC or `본인인증`, which is why they were admitted — but none should be presented as "no ID needed".
