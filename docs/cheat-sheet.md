## 🛵 Delivery to Your Hotel (No ARC)

- **Shuttle Delivery** — 100% English, US cards. Buy discounted e-gift vouchers on Klook first (~10–15% off).
- **Baemin** — English UI + Guest Checkout (`비회원 주문`); first-order banner coupons ~₩10,000 off; US Visa/MC/Amex/Apple Pay.
- **No tipping. No ID on delivery** (don't order alcohol — that triggers an age check).

## 🪪 The ONLY Times You'll Show Your Passport

1. **Buying alcohol** (age check) — or just skip the soju.
2. **Immediate Tax Refund at hypermarkets** (Lotte Mart / E-Mart, spend ≥₩15,000, save ~6–7%).
3. **Airline boarding-pass discounts** (name match) & **airport lounges** (card + boarding pass).

Everything else — restaurants, cafes, kiosks, markets, delivery — **your passport stays in your pocket.**
