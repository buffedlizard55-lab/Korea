# 🌊 Busan Guide — Cheap Eats by Area

Busan is Korea's second city — seafood capital, coastal scenery, and food prices often *lower* than Seoul. Every item below is US-tourist usable (✅), no ARC, no `본인인증`.

> **Last verified: 2026-08-03** · If your itinerary extends south (e.g., Nov 17–22). Also see: [Neighborhood Guide (Seoul)](neighborhood-guide.md) · [Visit Busan Pass section](tourist-promotions.md)

---

## 🚆 Getting There & Around

- **KTX from Seoul Station** → Busan Station: ~2.5 hrs (~₩59,800 standard, book via Klook/Korail for discounts).
- **Subway** (1–4 lines) with T-money/WOWPASS; taxis are cheap and abundant.
- **Best bases:** **Seomyeon** (central, subway hub, endless cheap eats) or **Haeundae** (beach, nicer hotels).

## 🗺️ By Area

### 서면 Seomyeon (central — best budget base)
- **Student/office lunch spots** — weekday lunch specials ₩7,000–9,500 (kimchi fried rice, pork cutlet, curry).
- **Dwaeji gukbap** alleys near the station — dozens of shops, ₩9,000–12,000.
- Budget coffee (Mega/Compose/Paik's) + Gimbap Cheonguk on every corner.
- International eats (Thai, pizza, burgers) for variety.

### 해운대/광안리 Haeundae · Gwangalli (beach + seafood)
- **Mipochip (해운대)** — famous haemuljang (soy-marinated seafood), **10% off with Visit Busan Pass** (orders of 2+). 🟡 [Trip.com field report](https://sg.trip.com/moments/detail/busan-432-144968511/)
- **Gwangalli** — the street facing the beach: Mexican, pizza, beer, and seafood; walk-and-compare prices.
- Haeundae traditional market — street food at local prices.

### 자갈치/국제시장/남포동 Jagalchi · Gukje · Nampo (old-town markets)
- **Jagalchi Fish Market** — Korea's largest seafood market; browse, compare stalls, eat fresh hoe (raw fish).
- **Gukje Market** & BIFF Square street food — try the famous seed hotteok (씨앗호떡).
- **Sulbing Gwangbok (bingsu cafe)** — **10% off everything with Visit Busan Pass** (order from staff, not the kiosk). 🟡 [Trip.com field report](https://sg.trip.com/moments/detail/busan-432-144968511/)

### 대학가 (PNU / Kyungsung Univ) — student districts
- Kimbap + soup combos and ₩6,000–8,000 sets, same `대학가` economics as Seoul's Hongdae/Sinchon.

## 🎫 Visit Busan Pass — quick recap

- 24h **₩49,000** / 48h **₩69,000**; free entry to 30+ attractions + ~50 discounted experiences (Busan X the Sky, Lotte World Busan, aquariums…).
- **Food discounts confirmed:** Sulbing Gwangbok 10%, Mipochip 10%, P.ARK Café & Bakery (Yeongdo) ice-cream discount.
- Buy on Klook (~US$32 for 24h) or the official app. [Full section here](tourist-promotions.md)

## ⚠️ Busan Notes

- **Jagalchi/Gukje:** bargaining is normal at market stalls — be friendly, compare prices first.
- **Convenience stores:** same CU/GS25/7-Eleven/emart24 **1+1/2+1 promos** as Seoul — they reset **Nov 1** nationwide.
- **Delivery:** Shuttle covers Busan (Seomyeon, Haeundae, Nampo); Baemin Guest Checkout works the same as Seoul.
- **Tax refunds:** Lotte Mart/E-Mart immediate refund ≥₩15,000 works in Busan too (passport at checkout).
