## 🔧 Corrections made

### 1. Tongin Market coin price was misleading (`SEOUL-TONGIN`)
- **Was:** "₩10,000 = 20 coins + tray" presented as *the* price, repeated in 5 documents.
- **Actually:** coins are **₩500 each**, sold from a **10-coin (₩5,000) minimum**. ₩10,000/20 coins is a
  comfortable one-person budget, not a fixed entry price. Two people do not need ₩20,000.
- **Source:** [Seoul Metropolitan Government mediahub](https://mediahub.seoul.go.kr/archives/2014553) ("1개의 엽전은 500원의 가치"), corroborated by the Dosirak Café's own guidance that 10 coins (₩5,000) per person is sufficient. *(An earlier draft cited the café's Naver `modoo!` microsite; that platform shut down on 2025-06-26, so the live official Seoul source is used instead.)*
- **Fixed in:** `cheat-sheet.md`, `city-deal-checklist.md`, `neighborhood-guide.md`, `trip-calendar.md`, `verification-log.md`.

### 2. Cheong Wa Dae is **not** bookable by ARC-less US tourists (`SEOUL-TOUR-CHEONGWADAE`) — highest-impact finding
- **Was:** logged `access=tourist`, "free entry; confirm reservation/on-site registration rules."
- **Actually:** admission is free, but **online reservation requires a foreigner registration number (ARC)
  and a Korean mobile number** — exactly the `본인인증` barrier this guide warns about everywhere else.
  ARC-less US tourists must use the **on-site foreigner walk-up desk** (Main Gate / Chunchu Gate),
  which has a **~2,000/day quota shared with 65+, disabled and veteran visitors**, and requires a passport.
  Also **closed Tuesdays**.
- **Why it matters:** this is the guide's own core thesis (Table 2 in `birthday-freebies.md` exists to flag
  ARC-blocked services) and Cheong Wa Dae slipped through as tourist-friendly.
- **Source:** official reservation guidance (`외국인 등록번호와 국내 휴대폰 번호가 있는 경우 예약 가능`; 현장발권 1일 2,000명),
  [MCST/korea.kr on the walk-up quota increase](https://www.korea.kr/news/policyNewsView.do?newsId=148914274).
- **Fixed in:** `data/deals.csv`, `city-research-hub.md`, `verification-log.md`.

### 3. No Brand Burger set price wrong
- **Was:** "sets from ₩6,400", "~169 locations".
- **Actually:** the Amazing Bulgogi **set is ₩4,500** (single ₩2,500). ₩6,400 is the *Amazing Double* set.
  The "169 locations" figure is not in the cited Shinsegae newsroom release.
- **Source:** [디지털타임스](https://www.dt.co.kr/article/12048737) and [코리아데일리](https://www.koreadaily.com/article/20260226070245404): "단품이 2500원, 세트가 4500원".
- **Fixed in:** `cheat-sheet.md`, `everyday-savings.md`.

### 4. Myeongryun Jinsa Galbi session times were inverted/mislabeled
- **Was:** cheat sheet said "Weekends: 100-min limit (vs 120 min weekday)" in the calendar but the
  headline row implied a flat "₩7,000–8,000 곤드레정식" weekday lunch as if nationwide.
- **Actually:** ₩21,900 adult and **weekday 120 min / weekend & holiday 100 min** are correct ✅, but the
  weekday lunch special is **branch-dependent** — real branch menus show 곤드레정식 ₩7,000, 불고기전골 ₩8,900,
  갈비탕 ₩11,900, 갈랭세트 ₩9,900. Presenting ₩7,000–8,000 as the standard is not safe.
- **Fixed in:** `cheat-sheet.md` (now states the range varies and must be confirmed at the branch).

### 5. Cheonan Tourism Taxi rates weren't attributed to the source that carries them
- The official `cheonan.go.kr` page advertises the 50% subsidy but **does not itemize the rates**.
  The 4h ₩80k / 8h ₩160k / +₩20k-per-hour figures come from Yonhap and other press. Now stated explicitly.
- ✅ The numbers themselves are **correct** — confirmed across [Yonhap](https://www.yna.co.kr/view/AKR20260224042200063),
  [국제뉴스](https://www.gukjenews.com/news/articleView.html?idxno=3512052), [서울신문](https://www.seoul.co.kr/news/society/2026/04/24/20260424500265).
- ⚠️ One caveat the repo missed: the "천안시 거주자" condition in the press applies to **taxi drivers being recruited**,
  not to passengers. The repo's "non-Cheonan residents" eligibility note should be re-checked at booking.

### 7. Busan Museum of Art is CLOSED, not free (`BUSAN-MUSEUM-ART`) — found on second pass
- **Was:** `status=active`, "regular admission free", listed as a reliable Busan anchor in the free-museum cluster.
- **Actually:** the **main building has been closed for a full renovation since December 2024**. The museum has
  announced an **autumn 2026 reopening** — which may or may not land before the Oct 31 – Nov 22 trip window.
  Only the **이우환공간 (Lee Ufan Space)** is open: Tue–Sun 10:00–18:00 (last entry 17:30), closed Mondays and
  Jan 1, and it **is free**. The ₩5,000 adult price still printed on the site is the suspended standard tariff.
- **Why it was missed:** the cited VISITKOREA listing still describes the museum in normal operation. Only the
  museum's **own** site carries the closure banner — a good example of why operator-level sources beat
  aggregated tourism listings for operational status.
- **Source:** [부산시립미술관 관람안내](https://art.busan.go.kr/index.nm?menuCd=1) ("본관 리노베이션으로 인해 ~2026년까지 장기휴관"),
  [CNB뉴스 / 코스미안뉴스 on the autumn 2026 reopening](https://www.cnbnews.com/news/article.html?no=771923).
- **Fixed in:** `data/deals.csv` (→ `live-check`), `data/deal-discovery-queue.csv`, `city-research-hub.md` (2 places).

### 6. Source-policy gap
- `hapskorea.com` (used by `BUSAN-LOCAL-FOOD`) was absent from `data/source-policy.csv`, so the classifier
  silently had no opinion on it. Added as `secondary`.
