# 🚫 What Not to Buy — Couple Anti-Tourist-Trap Rules

The cheapest decision is often declining a “deal” that creates extra spending, travel, or stress.

> **Last verified: 2026-08-05** · Use with [Effort vs. Savings Score](effort-value-score.md) and [Normal Local Backup Plan](backup-plan.md).

## Do not buy these without proof

| Avoid | Why | Better move |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A city pass before itinerary math | Included attractions may not fit the activation window | Build the real day, then run pass math |
| Extra products to reach 1+1/2+1 | Unwanted items are not savings | Buy only the exact tagged quantity you would use |
| Extra grocery items for tax refund | A refund does not make unwanted purchases valuable | Buy the planned list only |
| A distant coupon redemption | Taxi/subway time can cost more than the discount | Use a nearby posted-price backup |
| An old screenshot/barcode | Coupon may be expired or branch-excluded | Open current app/chat terms at the counter |
| Resident-only loyalty app setup | ARC/`본인인증` often blocks it | Use tourist-screened channels or walk-in value |
| Airport snack/gift haul by default | Airport convenience can carry a premium | Make one city supermarket stop on an existing route |
| Pass price based on “maybe” attractions | Pass math becomes fictional | Count only must-do attractions |
| A complicated payment card for tiny cashback | Loading/refunding/detour effort can erase value | Pay normally unless the merchant is already on route |
| A hotel solely because of one nearby deal | Hotel location affects every day | Use Hotel Scorecard and city anchors |

## Couple rule of thumb

If both of you would spend more than **15–20 minutes** or take a special detour for a small coupon, treat the saving as zero unless the effort/detour score still says it is strong.

## Before every purchase

1. Is this a normal price, live coupon, pass, payment cashback, or free attraction?
2. Is it active/live now?
3. Does it fit today’s route?
4. Would we buy it without the promotion?
5. What is the local backup?

If any answer is unclear, choose the nearby normal-priced option.
