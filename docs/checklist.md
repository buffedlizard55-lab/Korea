## ✈️ Pre-Trip (do before you fly)

### By September 30, 2026 (D-30)
- [ ] **IKEA Family Korea signup** — *each traveler*, at [ikea.com/kr/en/ikea-family/](https://www.ikea.com/kr/en/ikea-family/):
  - Use a **separate email per person**, set the **real birthday**.
  - **Turn marketing + email consent ON** in account settings — no consent = no birthday coupon (official FAQ).
  - ✅ Confirmed sent: coupon goes out **exactly 7 days before** each birthday; check **spam/promotions folders** too.
- [ ] **KakaoTalk** installed on your US phone, US number verified.
- [ ] Open the [**City Deal Checklist**](city-deal-checklist.md): confirm **Cheonan** as the third city (or replace it), save your hotel neighborhoods, and mark the Seoul/Busan deal rows you actually want to use.
- [ ] For every rotating coupon, keep the **barcode + expiry + participating branch** together; do not treat a general channel membership as a guaranteed coupon.
- [ ] Add brand channels (search in KakaoTalk): `@버거킹` · `@KFC코리아` · `@롯데리아` · `@배스킨라빈스` · `@던킨` · `@파리바게뜨` · `@뚜레쥬르` · `@네네치킨` · `@BBQ치킨` · `@맘스터치`
- [ ] Bookmark **Klook / KKday / Creatrip** dining vouchers (BBQ, buffets, cakes) — buy in USD.

### By October 15–20, 2026 (D-15)
- [ ] Install **Shuttle Delivery** (English) — buy **discounted e-gift vouchers on Klook** first (needs the Shuttle app to redeem).
- [ ] Install **Baemin** — set **phone OS language to English** (app follows the OS); plan to use **Guest Checkout (`비회원 주문`)**.
- [ ] Install **Naver Map** (English mode) + **Papago** + **H.Point Global** + **WOWPASS** (if using).
- [ ] Check [`koreasalefesta.kr`](https://www.koreasalefesta.kr) — official site returns around now (offline between editions).
- [ ] Note your **hotel address in Korean** (copy from Naver Map) — needed for Baemin delivery.

### By October 30, 2026 (D-1)
- [ ] **Screenshot the [Cheat Sheet](cheat-sheet.md)** for the flight / phone lock screen.
- [ ] Confirm any **early-November birthdays**: IKEA coupons should have arrived (D-7 before birthday).
- [ ] Pack: **refillable water bottle**, US credit cards (no-FTF), ~₩50,000–100,000 cash for markets, passport (of course!).
- [ ] Print or save this checklist — mark things off as you go.

## 🛬 In-Trip Daily Routine (5 minutes, every morning)

- [ ] **Open the [Trip Calendar](trip-calendar.md)** — what's on today (promo resets, closures, festival windows)?
- [ ] **Check KakaoTalk channel chats** — new monthly/seasonal coupon barcodes drop at month start.
- [ ] **Decide meal plan** with the [Neighborhood Guide](neighborhood-guide.md) (or [Busan Guide](busan-guide.md) if south), then update the matching row in the [City Deal Checklist](city-deal-checklist.md).
- [ ] Carry: passport only on days you'll buy alcohol, use hypermarket tax refund, or redeem the IKEA tart.

### Arrival night — Oct 31 (Sat)
- [ ] Convenience-store run: **last day of October's 1+1/2+1** — grab water, snacks, breakfast items.
- [ ] First delivery order (Shuttle or Baemin guest) to test the flow — **no tipping**.

### Nov 1 (Sun)
- [ ] **November promo reset** — new 1+1/2+1 at CU, GS25, 7-Eleven, emart24.

### Birthday days (Oct 31 – Nov 22)
- [ ] IKEA apple-tart coupon: **bring your passport** (some branches check ID for the tart).
- [ ] Free weekday hot drink at IKEA restaurant — **no ID needed** (weekdays only).
- [ ] Klook/KKday QR voucher for the celebratory dinner.

### Departure day — Nov 22 (Sun)
- [ ] **Airport lounge** (Priority Pass: Matina T1/T2, Sky Hub) or Klook airport meal voucher.
- [ ] **Tax refunds**: customs stamp on receipts (if not immediate), then **self-service kiosks at ICN T1/T2** → cash/card.
- [ ] Mark **field notes** ([template](field-notes.md)) — what worked, what didn't, prices paid.
