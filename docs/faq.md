# ❓ FAQ — Quick Answers for US Tourists in Korea

The questions travelers actually ask before and during the trip. All answers are consistent with the rest of this guide (US citizens, no ARC, Oct 31 – Nov 22, 2026).

> **Last verified: 2026-08-03**

---

## 🪪 Identity & Verification

**Q: Will I be asked for my passport to use any of these deals?**
A: Only in 3 situations: (1) buying alcohol (age check), (2) hypermarket Immediate Tax Refund at Lotte Mart/E-Mart checkouts (spend ≥₩15,000), (3) boarding-pass name-match & airport lounges. Restaurants, cafes, kiosks, markets, and delivery — no ID.

**Q: What is `본인인증` and why does it block me?**
A: It's Korea's telecom-based identity verification (PASS). It checks a Korean RRN/ARC against a Korean mobile contract. A US passport, US number, or tourist eSIM can't complete it — so native Korean loyalty apps (Starbucks KR, Outback, CJ ONE, Happy Point, Coupang Eats) are **off-limits**. Use the ✅ tourist-friendly channels in this guide instead.

**Q: Can I just use my US Starbucks app in Korea?**
A: No. Starbucks Korea is a separate rewards program and requires `본인인증`. Drink the ₩1,500–2,000 iced Americanos at Mega/Compose/Paik's/The Venti instead.

## 📱 Phone, eSIM & Apps

**Q: Does KakaoTalk work on a US phone number / tourist eSIM?**
A: Yes — install KakaoTalk, verify with your US number (SMS or call), then add brand channels (`채널 추가`) to receive barcodes. No Korean number needed.

**Q: Which apps should I have installed before the trip?**
A: KakaoTalk, Shuttle Delivery, Baemin (switch to English UI), Klook + KKday + Creatrip (book vouchers in USD), H.Point Global, Naver Map (English mode — Google Maps is unreliable for directions/transit in Korea), Papago (translation).

**Q: Do I need a Korean SIM/eSIM with a Korean number?**
A: No. A data-only tourist eSIM is enough. None of the ✅ deals require a Korean phone number. (Note: Baemin full account signup needs a Korean number, but Guest Checkout `비회원 주문` does not.)

## 💳 Payment

**Q: Will my US credit card work?**
A: Yes — Visa/Mastercard/Amex are accepted at kiosks, restaurants, and delivery apps (Shuttle, Baemin Guest Checkout). Carry a little cash (₩50,000–100,000) for markets (Tongin coins are cash-bought) and street stalls. Consider WOWPASS (prepaid KRW card) for cashback offers + a second payment method.

**Q: Do I need to tip?**
A: No. Korea has no tipping culture for restaurants, delivery, taxis, or hotels. Menu price is the final price.

**Q: Foreign transaction fees?**
A: Use a no-FTF card. Klook/KKday let you pre-pay in USD, which avoids surprise conversion rates.

## 🍽️ Eating & Deals

**Q: Is tap water safe to drink?**
A: Yes — Seoul's Arisu is heavily filtered and tested. Free Arisu refill stations are in subway stations across the city; bring a refillable bottle. Restaurants serve free water (usually cold).

**Q: Do kiosks have English?**
A: Most fast-food and coffee kiosks (`키오스크`) have an English button (often 🇬🇧/EN icon). If not, staff will help — pointing at the menu photo works fine.

**Q: Are these prices really "no membership needed"?**
A: For walk-in deals (Myeongryun Jinsa Galbi, Dookki, No Brand, Mom's Touch, Gimbap Cheonguk, Hong Kong Banjum, convenience stores, markets) — yes, just walk in. For coupon deals (IKEA Family, KakaoTalk channels, H.Point, Klook vouchers) — sign up once with email/foreign card, then scan the barcode.

**Q: The blog said X app gives a free birthday burger — why isn't it in this guide?**
A: Because it needs `본인인증` (Korean ID verification). This guide only lists what actually works for US citizens without an ARC. If you find a new one that works, add it with a source (see CONTRIBUTING.md).

**Q: Korea Sale Festa 2026 dates aren't published yet — what do I do?**
A: The 2025 festival ran Oct 29 – Nov 16 (2024: Nov 9–30), so your trip window is in the expected zone. Check `koreasalefesta.co.kr` in late September/October for the official 2026 dates and partner list.

## 🏨 Delivery & Hotels

**Q: Can I order food to my hotel?**
A: Yes — Shuttle Delivery (100% English) or Baemin Guest Checkout. Meet the driver in the lobby (most hotels don't let drivers upstairs). No ID check; don't add alcohol (age-check required).

**Q: What if my hotel has no English name on delivery apps?**
A: Shuttle is built for expats/tourists and handles hotel addresses in English. On Baemin, use the landmark/hotel search in the English UI, or enter the Korean address from Naver Map.

## 🧾 Tax Refunds

**Q: Can I get a tax refund on restaurant meals?**
A: No — meals and cafes are not tax-refundable. Tax refunds apply to retail shopping and hypermarket groceries/snacks ≥₩15,000 (Immediate Tax Refund ~6–7% at Lotte Mart/E-Mart registers, passport required).

**Q: What about duty-free?**
A: Duty-free shopping (airport or downtown duty-free stores) works for souvenirs/beauty — show your passport and flight info. Not relevant to food, but pair it with your last-day shopping run.

## 🗓️ Timing

**Q: Why do you keep mentioning November 1st?**
A: Convenience-store 1+1/2+1 promotions reset on the 1st of every month. You arrive Oct 31 (October's slate) and wake up Nov 1 to a fresh set — it's the rare trip where you catch two promo cycles in 24 hours.

**Q: Tongin Market — what if my only free day is Monday?**
A: It's closed Mondays and the 3rd Sunday (Nov 15). Use Mangwon Market (₩1,500 croquettes) or Namdaemun Market instead.
