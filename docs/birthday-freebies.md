## 🗽 How US Citizens Claim Birthday Rewards Without an ARC & Without Showing ID

When researching Korean birthday freebies online, most travel guides fail to mention that native Korean loyalty apps require **`본인인증` (PASS mobile identity verification)**—a system that checks a Korean resident ID against a domestic mobile carrier contract.

As a group of **US citizens without a Korean resident companion**:
1. **You cannot sign up for native Korean loyalty apps** like Starbucks Korea, Outback Boomerang, CJ ONE (VIPS), or Happy Point.
2. **Carry your passport only when the deal needs it:** ordinary barcode/QR redemption usually does not need it, but tax refunds, alcohol, boarding-pass checks, and the IKEA tart caveat are exceptions. A branch can apply its live terms.
3. **Your Birthday Action Plan:** Use **Table 1** below for your birthday celebrations between Oct 31 and Nov 22. Ignore **Table 2**, which lists resident-only apps.
