# 🎂 Birthday freebies in South Korea

Verified against official membership pages where possible. Korean brands change these often
(Outback and CGV both reworked theirs recently), so **re-check in-app before the meal**.

> Last verified: 2026-08-03

## ⏰ The single most important rule

Most Korean birthday coupons are **auto-issued 5–15 days before your birthday** and only
when you already meet the membership conditions:
- **Starbucks**: join **+30 days** beforehand
- **CJ ONE / VIPS**: coupon issued 15 days before
- **Twosome**: auto-issued 14 days before
- **Baemin/others**: phone-verified account

Plan like this if someone's birthday falls during the trip:

| Deadline | Action |
| --- | --- |
| **D-30+** | Sign up for Starbucks KR, register a card, earn ★5 → Green grade |
| **D-15** | Join CJ ONE, Outback Boomerang, Twosome Hearts, Happy Point, KFC / Burger King / Lotte Eatz apps; turn ON marketing consent (many coupons require SMS/email opt-in) |
| **B-day month, 1st** | Download newly issued coupons (Baskin Robbins, Burger King) |
| **Birthday week** | Use everything — most expire within 14–45 days |

## ⚠️ Known changes / gotchas (2025–2026)

- **Outback retired its birthday coupon** — the anniversary coupon (above) is the replacement.
- **CGV's free birthday combo is gone** — 50% *discount* coupon via CJ ONE for everyone; free combo only for VIP+ via the CGV app.
- **Marketing consent is a hidden gate**: several brands (e.g., Outback's PLATINUM birthday service, Nike/Adidas, IKEA) won't send coupons with SMS/email opt-out. Toggle it on ≥7 days before the birthday.
- **Almost every Korean brand app requires 본인인증** (Korean phone registered under your legal identity). Tourists usually can't pass this without a Korean-resident friend or an ARC — plan accordingly (label 📱 each row).
- **Starbucks Korea membership is separate** from the US/global program — stars and the app don't transfer.
