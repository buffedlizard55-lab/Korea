## 🏦 Step 1 — Get Money & Connectivity at ICN

Land at Incheon Terminal 1 or 2; these are all in the arrivals hall:

| What | Where / How | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **WOWPASS card** | Self-service kiosks in arrivals (T1 & T2) | Prepaid KRW card: works as **T-money transit**, top up with US card, and earns **partner cashback** (GS25 5%, Compose 5%, Dunkin 5%, Paik's 3%…). [wowpass.io](https://www.wowpass.io) |
| **T-money / transit card** | CU/GS25/7-Eleven in arrivals or subway machines | The cheapest way to ride subway/bus; ~₩4,000 for the card + top-up |
| **Cash** | Exchange desks at arrivals (rates worse than Myeongdong) | Get a small ₩50,000–100,000 for markets & street stalls; exchange the rest in [Myeongdong](money-guide.md) |
| **eSIM / SIM** | Buy online before the trip (airport pickup or QR) | Data-only eSIM is enough — **no Korean phone number needed** for any deal in this guide |
| **AREX / Airport Railroad** | Express train to Seoul Station (43 min) | Cheapest fast option; or K-Limousine airport bus, or taxi (~₩60–80k) |

> 🚇 **Tip:** if you got a Discover Seoul Pass, the mobile pass includes a **free 5-day SKT eSIM + free AREX** — activate before departure.

## 🧾 Step 2 — Tax Refunds on the Way Home (Nov 22)

Two kinds of refund, don't mix them up:

1. **Immediate Tax Refund (`즉시환급`)** — done at the store (Lotte Mart/E-Mart, ≥₩15,000, passport shown). Nothing to do at the airport.
2. **Airport refund** — for stores that only gave you a **Tax Free receipt** (Global Blue / Global Tax Free / Ktours etc.):
   - **Before security:** take purchases + receipts + passport to the **Customs desk** for a stamp/scan.
   - **After security (T1 & T2):** use the **self-service refund kiosks** — scan receipts/passport, choose **cash (KRW) or card refund**. Kiosks support English/Chinese/Japanese.
   - 🟡 [arirangbubu.com tax-refund guide (Apr 2026)](https://arirangbubu.com/survival-guide-korea-tax-refund-tourists/) · [airport.online ICN customs/refund](https://airport.online/seoul-incheon-airport/en/customs-and-tax-refund)
   - ⚠️ **Restaurant meals & cafes are never tax-refundable** — only retail shopping & hypermarket groceries ≥₩15,000.
