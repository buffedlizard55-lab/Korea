# 🛒 Grocery & Souvenir Plan — Buy Once, Not Randomly

A planned supermarket stop is usually better value than multiple tourist-zone snack and souvenir purchases. This plan is for a couple traveling through **Incheon, Seoul, Busan, and flexible Cheonan/Daejeon**.

> **Last verified: 2026-08-04** · Tax-refund rules: [official VISITKOREA guide](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/contents/contentsView.do?vcontsId=140736) · Status/source record: `NATIONWIDE-TAXREFUND`

## The one-stop rule

Make **one planned grocery/souvenir stop** near a route you already have. Do not add unwanted products merely to reach a tax-refund threshold.

| City | Best timing | What to do | Do not do |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Incheon** | Arrival only for necessities; departure only if time remains | Buy emergency water/snacks or use airport tax-refund steps for qualifying receipts | Buy bulky gifts at airport just because it is convenient |
| **Seoul** | Seoul Station / a nearby hypermarket on a transit day | Do a single snack/gift haul; bring passport if seeking immediate refund | Make a separate cross-city trip only for a supermarket |
| **Busan** | Near your actual base or before departure from Busan | Compare a local Lotte/E-Mart/large store with your planned route; check Busan Pay live merchant terms | Assume Busan Pay cashback or a tax refund applies at every store |
| **Cheonan / Daejeon** | Only if the flexible-city itinerary already has a supermarket nearby | Buy city-specific snacks only if you want them, then use normal posted price/tax rules | Let souvenirs decide which flexible city to choose |

## Couple packing list

| Buy category | Good use | Avoid |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Breakfast/snacks | 2–3 transit mornings, long rail days, airport departure | Buying a week’s worth that must be carried city to city |
| Gifts | Packaged, sealed, lightweight items with visible prices | Fragile food, unknown storage needs, oversized boxes |
| Drinks | One refillable bottle + only necessary drinks | Airport-price bottled water unless unavoidable |
| Convenience promotions | Items you already planned to buy in the exact tagged quantity | Adding unwanted items for a 1+1/2+1 “saving” |
| Beauty/retail | Only on a deliberate shopping day with passport/tax rules ready | Treating a tax refund as a reason to overspend |

## Immediate tax-refund reality check

For qualifying immediate-refund stores, official guidance says:

- present a **passport** at payment,
- a single qualifying payment must be **₩15,000 or more and under ₩1,000,000**,
- total immediate-refund purchases are subject to trip limits,
- goods must be qualifying/taxable and unused for export,
- a store must participate—look for the tax-refund sign or ask before paying.

Restaurant meals, ordinary café spending, and services are not a grocery tax-refund strategy.

## Ten-minute store routine

1. Check the live store tax-refund sign/merchant rules.
2. Add only items already on the couple list.
3. Check shelf promos for exact quantity and eligible flavors.
4. Compare the final normal total to the promo/tax benefit—do not guess.
5. Use the passport only if claiming tax refund.
6. Photograph the receipt and record the real savings in [Couple Budget](couple-budget.md).

## Normal fallback

If the store is too far, tax refund does not apply, or the promotion is confusing: buy the few items you need at the nearest convenience store or supermarket at the posted price. A small normal purchase is better than a long detour or unnecessary minimum spend.
