# 🌙 City-Night Allocation Planner

Before passes, branch cards, or detailed restaurant research, decide how many nights belong in each city. Too many hotel changes erase small deal savings.

> **Last verified: 2026-08-05** · Trip: Oct 31–Nov 22, 2026 · Couple itinerary with a flexible Cheonan/Daejeon leg.

## Start with fixed constraints

| Constraint | Planning implication |
| --- | --- |
| Incheon arrival/departure | Keep arrival/departure plans light; do not schedule a major pass that day unless timing supports it |
| Seoul | Needs clustered neighborhood days for Climate Card/DSP value |
| Busan | Needs enough nights to justify the long transfer and any VBP day |
| Cheonan/Daejeon | Choose **one** flexible city unless live transport/hotels make both genuinely worthwhile |
| Hotel changes | Treat each move as a cost in time, luggage effort, and possible taxi/transit spending |

## Planning rules

1. Give a city a pass only when the pass activation window fits consecutive, dense activity days.
2. Do not move hotels for a small food deal.
3. Give Busan enough time for the long Seoul–Busan transfer to feel worthwhile.
4. Give the flexible city at least one full anchor day or skip it.
5. Use free/walk-in-value anchors on transfer days; save pass-heavy days for settled hotel days.
6. A couple often benefits more from one fewer hotel move than from one additional marginal discount.
