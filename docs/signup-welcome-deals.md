- Paying with the registered card also gets **1 free Extra** (shot/syrup/drizzle…) per visit
- 🔗 Official: <https://www.starbucks.co.kr/msr/msreward/level_benefit.do>
- 📱 Korean phone verification; note: KR membership is **separate** from US/global Starbucks

## 🧾 First-time app tips

- Use **invite/referral links** when signing up for delivery apps — first-order packs via referrals are consistently larger (e.g., Coupang Eats ₩20k vs ₩10k).
- "신규" = fresh phone number **and** device. Reinstalling an app doesn't reset eligibility.
- Coupons typically expire **7–30 days** after issue — join apps a few days before you plan to order, not months early.
- The first-order coupon often requires a **minimum spend** (e.g., Baemin ₩15,000). Fried chicken (~₩20k) clears it easily.
- Stack: first-order coupon + pickup discount + club free-delivery = often 30–60% off a first meal.
