- **Welcome Hack:** Before ordering, buy **Shuttle Delivery e-gift vouchers at an exclusive discount on Klook** (check the current % — it varies by promo) and apply the code at checkout!

- **What it offers residents:** ₩10,000 off coupon issued the day after joining (min. ₩70,000 order), wine 2-for-₩9,000, and 10% discount or 2% points on every visit.

- **Why US Tourists should skip:** Starbucks Korea Rewards is independent from US Starbucks and requires `본인인증` to register an account.
- 🟢 [Official terms](https://www.starbucks.co.kr/msr/msreward/level_benefit.do)

## 🧾 First-Time Ordering & Welcome Tips for Your Trip

- **Use KakaoTalk Channels First:** Before walking into a burger or chicken joint, check your KakaoTalk chat rooms for new barcode coupons.
- **Stack Shuttle Deals:** Buy discounted Shuttle e-gift vouchers on Klook + use first-order referral promo codes for maximum delivery savings.
- **No Tipping in Korea:** Neither restaurants nor delivery drivers expect or accept tips. Your menu price or checkout total is exactly what you pay.
